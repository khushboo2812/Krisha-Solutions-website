About us

About Krisha Solutions

A Nairobi-based IT services and consulting business that helps organisations manage, maintain and improve their technology.

Who we are

All your IT needs under one roof

Krisha Solutions provides IT services, technical support, infrastructure management, software solutions and IT consulting to businesses in Nairobi and elsewhere in Kenya.

Our tagline, All Your IT Needs Under One Roof, describes what we set out to be: one dependable partner for the technology a business relies on, from the network and servers in the office to cloud services, business software, websites and equipment.

We work with businesses as a long-term IT partner. That means getting to know your environment, taking responsibility for the work we are asked to do, and helping you make sensible technology decisions over time, not just appearing when something breaks.

Meet the founder

Khushboo Ruparel

Founder and Director, Krisha Solutions

Khushboo Ruparel is the Founder and Director of Krisha Solutions, bringing nearly a decade of experience in the IT industry and a passion for helping businesses make technology work for them.

With a B.Sc. in Computing and experience across IT operations, software solutions and technology implementation, Khushboo founded Krisha Solutions to provide businesses with practical, reliable and cost-effective IT support tailored to their needs.

Her approach combines technical understanding with a business-focused mindset, helping organisations manage their IT infrastructure, implement technology solutions and navigate their IT challenges with confidence.

Through Krisha Solutions, Khushboo is building a trusted technology partner for businesses that need dependable IT expertise without the complexity of managing everything themselves.

Her vision

To make reliable, effective IT solutions accessible to businesses of all sizes, helping them work smarter, operate efficiently and grow with confidence.

Who we work with

Established businesses that depend on their IT

We mainly work with small and medium-sized businesses of around 20 to 150 employees, in Nairobi and elsewhere in Kenya. Many don’t have a dedicated internal IT team and need a reliable partner to look after their technology.

  • Accounting & audit firms

A realistic promise

No IT provider can promise that systems will never fail or that security incidents can never happen. What we can do is reduce avoidable problems through good maintenance and planning, respond in a structured way when issues occur, and keep you informed along the way.
