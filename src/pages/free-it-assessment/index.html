What we look at

Five areas of your IT infrastructure

The assessment covers the parts of your IT that keep your business running day to day.

Network and Wi-Fi

How your network and Wi-Fi are set up, and where the connection, coverage or cabling could be causing problems.

Servers and infrastructure

The servers, racks and core equipment your business depends on, and the condition they are in.

Security and firewalls

How your network is protected at the edge, and whether firewall and access settings are being looked after.

Backup monitoring

Whether your important data is being backed up, and whether anyone is checking that the backups actually work.

Preventive maintenance

Whether your systems get regular attention, or are only dealt with once something breaks.
