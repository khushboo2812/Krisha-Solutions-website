The problem

Supporting many clients shouldn’t mean juggling many inboxes

Requests scattered everywhere

Support requests arrive across several inboxes and spreadsheets, so there’s no single picture of what’s open.

Targets checked too late

Service targets are only looked at after they’ve been missed, when it’s too late to do anything about them.

No clear owner

Nobody is clearly responsible for each ticket, so requests wait while everyone assumes someone else has it.

How it works for an MSP

One account. Every client kept separate.

Run support for all your clients from one place, while each client sees only what belongs to them.

  1. One account, many organisations

    You have one KrishaSure account, and each client is its own organisation inside it.

  2. Each client set up their way

    Every client gets its own support email address, business hours and service targets (SLAs). Clients on a support contract also get contracted hours with a running balance.

  3. Clients raise and follow tickets

    Clients email their support address or log in to raise and follow tickets, and only ever see their own.

  4. One shared queue

    Your team works from a single shared queue across all clients.

  5. Suppliers, limited

    Outside suppliers can be given access to just their own category, and nothing else.

Automation

The routine work happens on its own

KrishaSure routes, times, escalates and follows up, so your team can focus on solving problems.

Routing

Tickets go to the right person by category skill and priority, using agent levels your company names itself, for example Tier 1 to Tier 3.

SLAs

Set targets by priority, category or client. They count business hours only, breaches are flagged, and agents get a heads-up at 75% of the target.

Escalation

A ticket that passes its SLA moves up to the next level with the right skill, and everyone involved is told.

Pending

While you wait on the client, the SLA clock pauses and the client is reminded. If they stay silent, the ticket closes itself.

Email

Clients, agents and admins get emails on key events (created, assigned, comments, escalated and resolved), sent from the client’s own support address. Replying by email adds to the same ticket, and an email to a support address becomes a ticket.

AI assistance

An AI assistant that helps before a ticket reaches your team

When someone raises a ticket, an AI assistant suggests a fix from the description and any screenshots, and answers follow-up questions.

  • If it doesn’t solve the problem, the whole conversation passes to the agent, so nobody has to start again.
  • Emailed tickets are categorised automatically, and an agent can correct the category in one click.
  • Each plan has a monthly AI allowance, so usage is capped and costs stay predictable.

FAQ

Common questions

Can clients see each other’s tickets?

No. Each client is its own organisation, and clients only ever see their own tickets.

Does it use business hours?

Yes. Each client has its own business hours, and SLA targets count business hours only.

What counts as a user?

Every active user in your account: your team, suppliers and client contacts. Deactivated users aren’t counted.

Can suppliers be limited?

Yes. A supplier can be given access to just their own category.
