KrishaSure

Structured ticketing for support requests. KrishaSure helps organisations handle support requests in an organised way: every request becomes a ticket that is assigned, tracked, resolved and available for reporting.

Why it matters

Support requests need structure

When requests arrive by phone call, WhatsApp, email and conversations in the corridor, it is easy for them to be forgotten, duplicated or left waiting with nobody clearly responsible.

Managers have little visibility of what is open, what has been resolved and where time is going. Staff don’t know whether anyone is working on their problem. KrishaSure addresses this by putting every request through the same structured workflow.

How it works

One workflow, from request to report

Every support request follows the same five stages.

  1. Ticket

    Each support request is recorded as a ticket, so nothing depends on someone remembering it.

  2. Assignment

    Tickets are assigned to the right person, so everyone knows who is responsible.

  3. Status tracking

    Each ticket shows where the request stands, so progress is visible rather than assumed.

  4. Resolution

    Requests are worked through to resolution, and the outcome is recorded on the ticket.

  5. Reporting

    Ticket records support reporting, giving you a clearer view of support activity over time.
