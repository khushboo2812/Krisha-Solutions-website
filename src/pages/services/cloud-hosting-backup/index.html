Cloud Services, Hosting and Backup

Help choosing, setting up and supporting cloud services and hosting, with backup solutions that are monitored, so problems are noticed early and not when you need to restore.

Discuss Cloud & Backup WhatsApp Us

The challenge

Your data needs to be there when you need it

Client files, financial records and project documents are often among the most valuable things a business owns.

Yet many businesses aren’t sure where all their important data is stored, whether it is backed up, or how long it would take to get back after a failure. Cloud services can help, but only when they are chosen and set up to suit the business.

Common concerns

  • Not knowing whether backups are running or complete
  • Important data kept on a single computer or server
  • Uncertainty about which cloud services suit the business
  • Hosting issues with nobody to resolve them

Ask a Question
Should we move everything to the cloud?

Not necessarily. Some systems suit the cloud well and others work better on-site. We’ll help you weigh up cost, internet connectivity and the way your team works before you decide.

How do we know our backups are working?

Backup monitoring shows whether each backup completes successfully. Periodic restore tests confirm that data can actually be recovered. We can help you put both in place.

Can you help with our website hosting?

Yes. Hosting support is part of this service, and we also build and maintain business websites. See website and e-commerce development.
