The challenge

Infrastructure problems affect everyone

When the network or a server has problems, the whole office feels it.

Many office networks have grown piece by piece: a switch added here, an access point there, a server that was never meant to stay in that cupboard. Over time this becomes harder to manage, harder to troubleshoot and more likely to cause disruption.

Common problems we help with

  • Slow or unreliable connections and Wi-Fi dead spots
  • Recurring outages with no clear cause
  • Cluttered racks and server rooms that are hard to work on
  • Moving offices or servers without a clear plan

Frequently asked questions

Can you work with our existing network?

Yes. Many projects start with an existing network. We look at what is already in place and recommend improvements, rather than replacing everything by default.

Can you help us move our servers to a new office?

Yes, server relocation is part of our infrastructure services. Planning the move carefully helps keep disruption to a minimum.

Our Wi-Fi is slow in parts of the office. Can you help?

We can look at coverage, configuration and how the network is used to find the cause, and recommend a practical solution.

Do you also provide ongoing support for the network?

Yes. Infrastructure support can be part of a managed IT services arrangement, so your network and servers are looked after on an ongoing basis.
