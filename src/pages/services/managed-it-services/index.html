The challenge

IT support that only reacts when something breaks

Without a structured arrangement, IT support tends to happen in emergencies.

Sound familiar?

  • IT problems are only dealt with once something breaks

  • Suppliers point at each other when something goes wrong

Frequently asked questions

What is managed IT support?

It is an ongoing arrangement in which an IT partner takes responsibility for supporting and maintaining your IT, instead of you calling a technician each time something goes wrong. The arrangement sets out what is covered and how support is provided.

We already have an IT person. Can you still help?

Often, yes. We can work alongside an internal IT person or small team, for example with infrastructure work, vendor coordination or extra support capacity. Tell us how your team is set up and we’ll discuss what would be useful.

Will managed IT support stop all IT problems?

No IT provider can honestly promise that. What regular maintenance and a structured approach do is reduce avoidable issues and make sure that, when problems happen, they are dealt with in an organised way.
